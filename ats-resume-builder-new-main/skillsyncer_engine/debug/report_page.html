    Why include the job title in your resume?

    It is recommended that you include the job title you are applying for in your resume, ideally in the summary statement at the top of your document. This is important for several reasons:

    • It shows the employer that you are specifically interested in the position and have tailored your application to their job opening.
    • It helps the employer quickly identify if your skills and experience match the requirements for the position.
    • It increases the likelihood that your resume will get noticed by the employer's applicant tracking system (ATS), which scans resumes for relevant keywords.
    • It demonstrates that you have a clear career goal and are focused on advancing in your chosen field.

    Overall, including the job title in your resume is a simple yet effective way to make a strong first impression and increase your chances of being invited for an interview.
