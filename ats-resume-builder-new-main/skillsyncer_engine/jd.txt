Job SummaryWe are seeking a detail-oriented Quality Assurance (QA) Specialist to monitor processes, test products or systems, and implement improvements to ensure deliverables meet consistent standards of quality. The ideal candidate will identify defects, enforce compliance, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to optimize our workflows and maintain high product standards.Key ResponsibilitiesProcess Monitoring: Audit workflows, inspect products or software, and verify compliance with industry and regulatory standards.Testing & Inspection: Design comprehensive test plans, create test cases, execute testing, and log defects or non-conformities.Root Cause Analysis: Investigate the underlying causes of product defects and coordinate corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) with engineering or production teams.Documentation: Track quality metrics, maintain audit reports, and document testing results for management review.Continuous Improvement: Train team members on quality control protocols and recommend process enhancements to reduce waste and boost efficiency.Requirements & SkillsAnalytical Thinking: Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills to troubleshoot issues and analyze data trends.Communication: Ability to collaborate clearly with engineers, project managers, and leadership to report non-compliance and track resolutions.Technical Knowledge: Familiarity with quality management frameworks (such as ISO 9001, Six Sigma, or industry-specific regulations) or testing tools.Education & Experience: A bachelor's degree in a relevant field (engineering, computer science, quality management) or equivalent practical experience in a quality-focused role.
